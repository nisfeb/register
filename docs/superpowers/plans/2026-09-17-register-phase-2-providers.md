## 1. Provider cores, pure, one file each, import-free like the lib

`code/lib/register-http.hoon`: `+url-encode |=(t=@t @t)` (percent-encode all but unreserved, uppercase hex, as calendar's `+enc-seg`); `+form-body |=(kvs=(list [@t @t]) octs)` (k=v joined by &, values encoded); `+basic-auth |=([user=@t pass=@t] @t)` answers `Basic <base64>` using `en:base64:mimes:html`; `+bearer |=(t=@t @t)`. Tests: encoding of a space, an ampersand and a unicode byte; a form body with two keys; basic auth of `a:b` is `Basic YTpi`.

`code/lib/register-stripe.hoon`: `+checkout-request |=([key=@t rid=@t email=@t lines=(list [name=@t cents=@ud qty=@ud]) success=@t cancel=@t expires=@ud] request:http)`: POST `https://api.stripe.com/v1/checkout/sessions`, headers authorization Bearer key and content-type form, body with `mode=payment`, `customer_email`, `metadata[rid]`, `success_url` (with the literal `{CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}` placeholder appended as `&sid={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}`, which must NOT be percent-encoded: add it after encoding), `cancel_url`, `expires_at`, and per line `line_items[i][price_data][currency]=usd`, `[price_data][product_data][name]`, `[price_data][unit_amount]`, `[quantity]`. `+session-request |=([key=@t sid=@t] request:http)`: GET `/v1/checkout/sessions/<sid>`. `+read-session |=(body=@t (unit [id=@t paid=? total=@ud rid=@t intent=@t url=@t]))`: `id`, `payment_status == "paid"`, `amount_total`, `metadata.rid`, `payment_intent` (a string or null), `url`. `+webhook-sid |=(body=@t (unit @t))`: the `data.object.id` of an event whose `type` begins `checkout.session.`; ~ otherwise. Tests: the request body contains every key in order; read-session on a paid fixture and an unpaid one; webhook-sid on a checkout event and on a `payment_intent.succeeded` event (~).

`code/lib/register-docusign.hoon`: `+auth-url |=([host=@t key=@t redirect=@t state=@t] @t)`: `<host>/oauth/auth?response_type=code&scope=signature&client_id=…&redirect_uri=…&state=…`. `+token-request |=([host=@t key=@t secret=@t grant=?(%code %refresh) value=@t redirect=@t] request:http)`: POST `<host>/oauth/token` with Basic auth of key:secret and the form `grant_type=authorization_code&code=…&redirect_uri=…` or `grant_type=refresh_token&refresh_token=…`. `+read-token |=(body=@t (unit [access=@t refresh=@t ttl=@ud]))`. `+userinfo-request |=([host=@t access=@t] request:http)` GET `<host>/oauth/userinfo`; `+read-userinfo |=(body=@t (unit [account=@t base=@t]))`: the account with `is_default` true, its `account_id` and `base_uri`. `+envelope-request |=([base=@t account=@t access=@t template=@t role=@t rid=@t name=@t email=@t party=@t children=@t] request:http)`: POST `<base>/restapi/v2.1/accounts/<account>/envelopes` JSON `{templateId, status: "sent", templateRoles: [{roleName, name, email, clientUserId: rid, tabs: {textTabs: [{tabLabel: "party", value: party}, {tabLabel: "children", value: children}]}}]}`. `+read-envelope-id |=(body=@t (unit @t))` (`envelopeId`). `+view-request |=([base=@t account=@t access=@t envelope=@t rid=@t name=@t email=@t return=@t] request:http)`: POST `…/envelopes/<envelope>/views/recipient` JSON `{returnUrl, authenticationMethod: "none", email, userName: name, clientUserId: rid}`. `+read-view-url |=(body=@t (unit @t))`. `+status-request |=([base=@t account=@t access=@t envelope=@t] request:http)` GET the envelope; `+read-status |=(body=@t @t)` its `status` (`sent`, `delivered`, `completed`, `declined`, `voided`, or `''`). Tests: each request's method, url and, for JSON ones, the decoded body's keys; each reader on a fixture.

`code/lib/register-mail.hoon`: `+send-request |=([key=@t from=@t to=@t subject=@t text=@t] request:http)`: POST `https://api.resend.com/emails` JSON `{from, to: [to], subject, text}` with Bearer key. `+read-send |=([status=@ud body=@t] (unit @t))`: the `id` when status is 200 or 201. `+render |=([cj=json template=@t vars=(list [@t @t])] [subject=@t body=@t])`: fills `email.<template>.subject` and `.body` from copy with `fill` (copy the two arms `+fill`/`+replace` here or take them from register.hoon; the lib files are separate cores, so copy). Tests: the request shape; render on a copy fixture.

## 2. Nexus (`code/nex/register/app.hoon`)

Imports: `/<  http  /lib/register-http.hoon`, `/<  stripe  /lib/register-stripe.hoon`, `/<  dsn  /lib/register-docusign.hoon`, `/<  mail  /lib/register-mail.hoon`.

**Outbound fetch with a deadline**, copied from calendar's `+fetch-hdr`: `+fetch |=(request:http (fiber ,[status=@ud headers=(list [@t @t]) body=@t]))`; a timer at two minutes; status 0 when nothing came. `send-request:io` and the timer need the ask to grow: `weir.json` gains poke `/sys/iris/` ("talk to Stripe to take payments, to DocuSign to take signatures, and to Resend to send the pilgrims their emails. Refuse this and nothing can be paid, signed or sent") and poke `/sys/behn/` ("give up on a provider that does not answer within two minutes"). Vendor `timer-set.hoon`, `timer-rest.hoon`, `timer-wake.hoon` from orrery's `code/mar/` (the blots the timer helpers lay). On `~wex`, re-approve the weir with the two new roads (the permits route as in phase 1 Task 3 step 6) and reload.

**Settings** gain `fees.suggested_full` (15000) and `fees.suggested_bambino` (5000) in `+starter-settings` and `+de-settings` (`+$ settings` fees becomes `[full bambino suggested-full suggested-bambino]`; every constructor updated; tests updated), and `docusign.role` ('Registrant') and `docusign.redirect` (computed: `<public_url>/apps/register/admin/docusign/return`, not stored). `+status-json` emits the suggested fees.

**DocuSign OAuth**, calendar's shape: `GET /api/admin/docusign/connect` (owner): refuses 400 when integration key or secret is empty; makes a random `state`, stores it in `/docusign-auth.json` (add the `%fall` row: `[%o ~]`), 302 to `+auth-url`. `GET /admin/docusign/return?code&state` (owner; eyre's login redirect applies): checks the state, exchanges the code with `+token-request %code`, on success stores `{access_token, refresh_token, expires_ms}`, then `+userinfo-request` and, when `docusign.account_id` or `base_uri` in settings is empty, writes them through `set-settings` (unmasked path: read settings, set the two keys, poke), 302 to `/apps/register/admin#settings?docusign=connected`; on failure 502 with the provider's error text. `+docusign-token`: like calendar's `+google-token`: a valid access token, refreshed with `+token-request %refresh` when within 60 seconds of expiry, `~` when not connected or the refresh fails; a refresh rotates the stored refresh token. `POST /api/admin/docusign/disconnect` clears the document. The settings view in the backoffice shows Connected or Not connected (read from a new `connected` boolean in `GET /api/admin/settings`, computed from a non-empty refresh token) and the Connect and Disconnect buttons.

**Sign, live** (`+serve-sign`, the `%live` branch that answered 501): token from `+docusign-token`, else 503 `'DocuSign is not connected'`. The registrant is the first person; `name` "First Last"; `party` every person as "First Last" joined by ", "; `children` the children's names the same way, or "none". Create the envelope (`+fetch`), read the id, else 502 with the status and the body's `message`. Poke `set-waiver` with `[%docusign envelope %sent ~]`. Then the recipient view with `returnUrl` `<public_url>/apps/register/sign/return?rid=<rid>&t=<token>`; answer `{"url": <view url>}`. If the registration already holds a `%docusign` envelope with status `%sent`, skip creation and ask a fresh view for it.

`GET /sign/return?rid&t` (public): `+with-reg` else 404; when status is not `%waiver`, 302 to `/apps/register/#next/<rid>/<t>` (already advanced by an earlier return). Else read the envelope status. `completed`: poke `advance` to `after-waiver` with waiver `[%docusign envelope %completed now]`, what `'signed the waiver'`, then 302 to `#next`. `declined` or `voided`: poke `set-waiver` with that status, 302 to `#next/<rid>/<t>?waiver=declined`; the page shows copy `next.waiver.declined` above the sign button when the waiver status is declined (the reg view already carries `waiver.status`). Anything else (`sent`, `delivered`, or a fetch failure): 302 to `#next/<rid>/<t>?waiver=pending`; the page shows `next.waiver.pending` ("We have not received the signed waiver yet. Give it a minute and reload, or sign again."). The event parameter DocuSign appends is ignored: the GET is the truth.

**Pay, live** (`+serve-pay`): the body may carry `amount` (cents, the total the pilgrim chose). Refuse 400 `amount: below the fee` when under `fees-total`. `gift` is `amount - fees`. Lines: `("<track title> registration", fee-per-person, people)` for the walkers and non-walkers alike (one line at the track fee with quantity = people), and when gift > 0 a line `("Gift to the Baby Steps Camino", gift, 1)`. Session with `customer_email`, `metadata[rid]`, `success_url` `<public_url>/apps/register/pay/return?rid=<rid>&t=<token>` (the `sid` placeholder appended by the builder), `cancel_url` `<public_url>/apps/register/#next/<rid>/<token>`, `expires_at` now + 24h in unix seconds. Poke a `note` op `stripe.session` with the session id in `why` (not a secret). Answer `{"url"}`. Stub mode unchanged, except it records `gift` from the body too.

`GET /pay/return?rid&t&sid` (public): `+with-reg`; if not `%payment`, 302 to `#next`. `+session-request` for `sid`; `+read-session`; when `paid` and `rid` matches: poke `advance` to `%complete` with payment `[%stripe fees gift now sid | '']` where `gift` is `total - fees` (0 if the total is below fees, which cannot happen but must not crash); then send the confirmation email (below); 302 to `#next`. Not paid: 302 to `#next/<rid>/<t>?pay=pending` and the page shows `next.payment.pending`.

`POST /hooks/stripe` (public, no auth): read the body with `+webhook-sid`; `~` answers 200 `{"ok": true}` at once. Else `+session-request`, `+read-session`; when paid and the named registration exists at `%payment`, poke the same `advance` as the return and send the confirmation; answer 200 `{"ok": true}` in every case, 200 even on a provider failure (Stripe retries on non-2xx; a failed GET is noted in the ring as `stripe.webhook` not ok). The webhook body is never trusted for anything but the id.

**Email**: `+send-mail |=([template=@t r=reg extra=(list [@t @t])] (fiber ,~))` in the nexus: reads settings and copy; vars `first` (first person's first name), `email`, `event` (settings.event.name), `link` (`<public_url>/apps/register/#manage/<rid>/<token>`), `site` (public_url), `position` (from `+position-of` over the loaded regs), `total` (`money` of fees), plus `extra`; `+render`; in stub mode, or when the Resend key is empty, poke `note` `email.<template>` ok with `why` `'stub: <subject>'`; in live mode `+fetch` the `+send-request` and poke `note` `email.<template>` with ok `?=(^ id)` and `why` the id or `'resend answered <status>'`. Never the body, never the link, in the ring. Send points: confirmation on every path to `%complete` (pay return, webhook, assist approve, admin `pay`); waitlist on a wait-listed submit (replacing phase 1's stub note); promoted on promote; assistance_approved and assistance_declined on assist; cancelled on cancel (pilgrim or admin); reminder and manage through the backoffice's `resend` op (the phase 3 stub becomes a real send; `manage` is the confirmation template). Copy already has every template; add `email.manage.subject` and `.body` ("Your Baby Steps Camino registration link": `{{link}}`).

**The page** (`public.js`): the pay step becomes a small chooser: three radio rows, "Registration fee {{total}}", "Full cost {{suggested}} (please consider covering the real cost of your pilgrimage)", "Another amount" with a dollars input, minimum the fee; the button posts `{amount}`; copy keys `next.payment.minimum`, `next.payment.suggested`, `next.payment.custom`, `next.payment.custom_help`. `next.waiver.declined`, `next.waiver.pending`, `next.payment.pending` rendered from the `?waiver=` and `?pay=` query on the `#next` route and from `waiver.status`.

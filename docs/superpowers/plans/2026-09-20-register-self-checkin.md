# Register: pilgrims check themselves in

sneagan, 2026-09-20: "design a way for people to check themselves in. on each day of the event and not before each pilgrim gets an email with a check in link. if they're solo they just get a button to check in. if they're in a group then they see check boxes to say who's there and then a check in button. they can't check in before the day of. admins get a live per-day check in list with % checked in etc." Then: "admins should be able to check people in like they do today but this is a fallback." Decisions taken in the design conversation: an organizer sends the day's email by a button in the backoffice each morning, not a timer; only complete registrations get a link. One implementer, one review, one fix pass. Recipes for `~wex` in `2026-09-17-register-phase-1.md` lines 30 to 95; the wex notes in `/project/fake-ships/wex-loom-full` and `/project/register/status` (the dojo is shared: check for the `> |commit` echo, and never type into a busy dojo).

## What changes for whom

- A pilgrim in a complete party gets an email on the morning of each event day with a link. On the day the link shows one button for a solo party, or a checkbox per person and one button for a group. Before the day it names the date. It never lets them undo anyone.
- Susan presses one button each morning to send that day's links, and opens one page to watch the day: how many are here, who is not, and a way to check a party in herself when their phone is dead.
- Sarah's volunteers keep the app exactly as it is. A self check-in shows up there as checked, by "pilgrim". Their taps still work and still hand out the wristband answer.

## 1. Library (`code/lib/register.hoon`, tests in `tests/lib/register.hoon`)

- Settings: `event.utc_offset_hours` (a signed integer, default `-5`: Eastern Standard Time, which is what December is) in `+starter-settings`; the typed `settings` gains `offset=@sd`; `+de-settings` reads it, missing or bad reads as `-5`. The Settings page shows it under Event as "Hours from UTC" with the help "-5 in December for Florida". Test: the default reads -5; `+7` and `-11` read back.
- `+event-day |=([days=(list @t) offset=@sd now=@da] (unit @tas))`: shifts `now` by the offset in hours, takes the date part as `YYYY-MM-DD` (the same text `+en-iso` writes, cut at 10), and answers `%fri` for the first day in `days`, `%sat` for the second, `%sun` for the third, `~` otherwise. `days` is the `event.days` list of settings.json, in order, as the check-in app already assumes. Tests: the day before is `~`; 00:30 UTC on 2026-12-05 is still `%fri` with offset -5 (it is 7:30pm Friday in Florida); 04:59 UTC on 2026-12-05 is `%fri`, 05:00 is `%sat`; the day after the last is `~`; a `days` list shorter than three still answers for the days it has.
- `+event-over |=([days=(list @t) offset=@sd now=@da] ?)`: true when the shifted date is after the last day. Test it.
- `+checkin-expected |=([regs=(list reg) day=@tas] @ud)`: the people in complete registrations who are there that day: `on-day` or `social-day`. A bus rider walks, so the bus adds nobody. `+checkin-done |=([regs=(list reg) day=@tas] @ud)`: the people in complete registrations with a check-in that day. Both skip a registration that is not `%complete`. Tests: a complete party of three with two walking Friday and one Sunday-only counts two on Friday; a checked-in person in a `%payment` party counts in neither.
- `+en-checkin-page |=([r=reg day=(unit @tas) over=? opens=@t] json)`: what the pilgrim's link reads. `status` (the registration's), `day` (`fri`, `sat`, `sun`, or `''`), `over`, `opens` (the next event date as text, `''` when none), `people`: a list of `{i, first, last, checked, at}` for the day, or `checked` false for every person when `day` is `~`. Test: a party of two with one checked on Friday.
- Copy, in `+starter-copy`: `checkin.title` "Check in", `checkin.early` "Check-in for {{day}} opens on {{date}}. Come back then.", `checkin.over` "The Camino is over for this year. Thank you for walking with us.", `checkin.gone` "This registration is no longer active. Please see the organizers at the start.", `checkin.solo.body` "Welcome, {{first}}. Press the button and a volunteer will give you your wristband.", `checkin.solo.button` "I'm here", `checkin.group.body` "Welcome. Tick everyone who is here today, then press the button. Someone who arrives later can use this link again.", `checkin.group.button` "Check us in", `checkin.done` "You're checked in. A volunteer at the start has your wristband.", `checkin.done.some` "{{names}} checked in. Anyone else can use this link when they arrive.", `checkin.nobody` "Tick at least one person.", `email.checkin.subject` "Check in for {{day}}'s walk", `email.checkin.body` "Good morning, {{first}}. When you reach the start today, open this link and check in: {{link}}. A volunteer will give you your wristband." The email is one of the templates the ship knows: `+known-template` (or the cord test in `+serve-resend-template`) gains `checkin`. Test: `test-copy` expects every new key; `test-with-starter` expects `email.checkin.body`.

## 2. Nexus (`code/nex/register/app.hoon`)

Public, token-checked with `+with-reg`, the token being the manage token the emails already carry:

- `GET /api/reg/<rid>/checkin?t=<token>`: reads settings, computes `+event-day` and `+event-over` at `now`, answers `+en-checkin-page`. A wrong token is a 404 like every other token route. The registration's status is in the answer; the page says "gone" itself when it is not `%complete`.
- `POST /api/reg/<rid>/checkin?t=<token>` with `{"people": [i, ...]}`: 404 on a bad token; 409 `{"error": "not today", "code": "early"}` when `+event-day` is `~` and the event is not over, 409 `code` `over` when it is; 409 `code` `gone` when the registration is not `%complete`; 400 when `people` is empty or names an index past the party. The day is the ship's, never the body's. One writer op `checkin` per index with `by` `'pilgrim'` and `undo` false, the same op the volunteers' batch pokes. Answers the page document again so the page paints the ticks from the ship's own copy. Idempotent by construction: `+with-checkin` keeps the first `at`.

Owner only, behind `act`:

- `POST /api/admin/checkin-mail` with `{"day": "fri", "again": false}`: 400 unless the day is one of the three. Loads every registration; the recipients are the `%complete` ones that have nobody on that day's check-in list yet and, unless `again`, have no history step whose `what` is `email.checkin.<day>`. Takes the first 100 of those in `by-last` order; for each: in stub mode, or while phase 2 has not landed, the `note` writer op with `what` `email.checkin.<day>`, `ok` true, `why` the filled subject, `by` `'stub'`, exactly as `+serve-resend-template` does, PLUS a history step on the registration, `what` `email.checkin.<day>`, so the next press skips it. A new writer op `mail-sent {rid, what, by}` appends that history line through `+note-hist` without touching anything else (the existing `note` op writes only the ring). Answers `{"sent": n, "remaining": m}` where `remaining` is how many recipients are left after this press. When phase 2 lands, the send happens where the stub note is, and a failed send skips the history line so a repress retries it.
- `mail-sent` answers `|` to the writer loop: no beacon bump per recipient, or every phone on the beach would re-read the roster a hundred times per press.
- `GET /api/checkin/roster?day=` is what the day view reads. It gains `expected` and `done` at the top level from the two new arms, beside `planned`. Nothing else changes; the volunteers' app ignores the two keys.

Routes: two `[%api %reg @ %checkin ~]` lines and one `[%api %admin %'checkin-mail' ~]` line in `+handle-request`. The writer's `+apply` gains `mail-sent`. No new road in `weir.json`.

## 3. The pilgrim's page (`public.js`, copy only in `public.css` if a rule is needed)

- Route `#checkin/<rid>/<token>`. Reads the page document; renders by its fields, in this order: status not `complete` → `checkin.gone`; `over` → `checkin.over`; `day` empty → `checkin.early` with `{{day}}` the weekday of `opens` and `{{date}}` its `dateWords`; one person → `checkin.solo.body` and the button, or `checkin.done` when already checked; more → `checkin.group.body`, a checkbox row per person (`first last`, ticked and disabled with "checked in at 7:42am" when `checked`), the button, and `checkin.done.some` naming the checked ones when any are. The button posts the ticked indexes; the solo button posts `[0]`. A 409 renders by its `code` the same way the GET fields do. Every wait shows the spinner; the answer repaints from the document the ship sends back.
- Edit text: a `checkin` step in `STEPS` (label "Check-in") rendering the group view from a fixture of two people, one checked, and `checkin.early` and `checkin.done` reachable under Other strings. `stepsHtml` count grows by one; the click-through count moves with it.
- The `#next` complete page gains nothing: the link comes by email on the day, and the manage page is not the place for it.

## 4. The backoffice (`admin.js`, `admin.html`, `admin.css`)

- Nav gains "Today", which opens `#day/<day>` for the event day it is now (from `event.days` and the browser's local date, the way the check-in app's `dayFor` does), or `#day/fri` outside the event. The view has a Fri, Sat, Sun switch.
- The count line: "{done} of {expected} expected Friday checked in, {percent}%", then "{n} parties not here yet". A party is expected that day when someone in it walks it or has its social, the ship's own rule (`+there-that-day`); the day's segments show only those, and a family that walks only Sunday is under "everyone, any day" on Friday. Segments: someone missing (the default, so a family stays on the list until the last of them is in), nobody here yet, everyone here, everyone. An empty list under the first two says "Everyone expected Friday is here." The search box, the same `matches` as the roster. The table: party (names, each with a tick when checked, "themselves" or the volunteer's name and the time in a muted line under the name), status badge, wristband answer, and a "Check in" button per person not yet checked plus "Check in everyone" for the party, which post to `/api/checkin` with the organizer's name exactly as the detail page's undo does. Optimistic: the tick paints at once, the read after confirms; the roster route is re-read on the beacon and every 60 seconds, never while the search box has the caret (the `render` focus rule from version 12 covers it).
- The send box at the top of the view: "Email Friday's check-in links", a checkbox "Send again to everyone who already got one" (kept in page state, not the DOM, so a repaint does not untick it), and a line that says what happened: the page presses the route until `remaining` is 0, waiting a moment between presses so the last party's line has landed, updating "Sent 100, 143 to go", then "Sent 243 links" or "Sent 0 links: everyone had one already, or is checked in." The button is enabled only on that day; on any other day's tab a second checkbox, "It is not Saturday yet. Send anyway", enables it, so a rehearsal or a mistaken tab cannot mail a day early by accident. The recipients are parties expected that day, so a Sunday-only family gets no Friday link. In stub mode the line starts "Rehearsal: no email leaves the ship." (the page knows `mode` from `/api/status`). A single family that never got the email is sent it from their record: `checkin` joins the detail page's template list, filled with the day and refused outside the event days, and it counts as sent.
- Reports: the per-day table's "Checked in" column already counts these; add "by themselves" as a second number in that cell.
- Settings: the "Hours from UTC" field under Event.

## 5. Proof

- Unit: the arms in section 1, and the day rule at every edge listed there. Expect 43 arms to become 48 or more.

## Not in this change

Undo from the pilgrim's link. A timer that sends the mail. GPS or a QR code at the start. Any change to the volunteers' app. Real email, which is phase 2: until then the button records what would have gone out, in the ring and in each registration's history, and says so.

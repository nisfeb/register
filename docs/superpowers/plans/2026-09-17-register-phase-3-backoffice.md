# Register Phase 3: the backoffice

**Goal:** the organizers' app at `/apps/register/admin`: roster, registration detail with every action, manual adds, reports, exports, backup and import, copy and settings editors, live through the beacon. Spec sections 6 and 9 (owner routes) are the requirements; this plan is the brief. Phase 2 (providers) follows; the actions that need a provider are stubbed here the way sign and pay are.

**Working rules:** one implementer, one review at the end. The recipes for `~wex` (cookie, fast loop, unit tests, forge) are in `2026-09-17-register-phase-1.md` lines 30 to 95. Global constraints of that plan hold. The gate runs in the foreground only, never two at once. Never touch `~ricsul-bilwyt`.

## 1. Library changes (`code/lib/register.hoon`, tests in `tests/lib/register.hoon`)

- `reg` gains two fields at the end: `exempt=?` (an organizer's mark: this party does not count against the track caps) and `prior=@tas` (the status a cancel left, for reinstate; `%$` when never cancelled). `stored-reg` becomes `[%2 =reg]`; keep `reg-1` and `stored-reg-1` as the old shape; `+read-reg` lifts `%1` to `%2` with `exempt=|` and `prior=%$`. `+new-reg` sets both. Test: a `%1` noun reads as `%2` with the defaults.
- `+tally`: an exempt party adds to `social-fri` and `social-sat` but to none of `full`, `bambino`, `late`. Test it.
- `+transition-ok`: `%cancelled` may go to `%waitlist`, `%waiver`, `%payment`, `%assistance` or `%complete` (reinstate restores the prior status). `+reinstate |=([r=reg by=@t now=@da] (unit reg))`: `~` unless `%cancelled` with a non-empty `prior`; else `set-status` back to `prior` with what `'reinstated'`. `+do-cancel` (nexus) sets `prior` before `set-status`. Tests: cancel then reinstate lands on the old status; reinstating a never-cancelled row is `~`.
- `+en-reg` emits `exempt` and `prior`. `+en-row |=([r=reg fees=@ud position=@ud] json)`: the roster row: `id, status, track, source, created, updated, email, phone, state, org, names` (a list of "Last, First"), `people` (count), `walkers` (count), `position, fees, paid` (payment method), `amount, waiver` (waiver status), `exempt, assistance, knight_dame` (any), `volunteer` (any), `nonwalker` (no walker in the party).
- CSV: `+csv-cell |=(t=@t @t)` quotes a cell when it holds a comma, quote or newline, doubling quotes. `+csv-people |=([regs=(list reg) s=settings] @t)`: header then one row per person with the party's fields repeated: `rid, status, track, source, created, updated, email, phone, street, city, state, zip, org, why, assistance, exempt, position, fees, payment_method, payment_amount, payment_gift, payment_at, payment_ref, refunded, waiver_method, waiver_status, waiver_at, notes, first, last, child, fri, sat, sun, sun_ten, social_fri, social_sat, mass_fri, holy_hour, bus, first_bsc, knight_dame, volunteer, checkin_fri, checkin_sat, checkin_sun`. `+csv-regs`: one row per registration, the party fields plus `people` and `walkers` counts and `history` (each step as `at by what` joined by `; `). Tests: a cell with a comma is quoted, a quote is doubled, the header column count equals every row's.
- Bundle: `+$ bundle [%1 regs=(list reg) settings=json copy=json counts=json]`. `+en-bundle |=(b=bundle json)` (regs through `+en-reg-full`, a new encoder that includes `token`, `exempt`, `prior` and every field, since a backup must round-trip) and `+de-bundle |=(j=json (unit bundle))` (the inverse; a registration that fails to decode makes the whole bundle `~`, with the failing id in a second `(each bundle @t)` variant `+de-bundle-why`). `+jam-bundle |=(b=bundle @)` is `(jam b)`; `+cue-bundle |=(a=@ (unit bundle))` is `(mole |.(;;(bundle (cue a))))`. Tests: jam then cue is identity; a truncated atom cues to `~`; JSON round trip is identity for a registration with history and check-ins.
- `+starter-copy` gains `admin.*` keys the backoffice needs only if it renders copy; it does not: the backoffice's strings are its own, in admin.js (organizers, not pilgrims). Skip.

## 2. Nexus routes (`code/nex/register/app.hoon`), all owner only, mutating ones behind `act`

- `GET /admin`, `/admin.css`, `/admin.js` serve `code/nex/register/admin.html`, `admin.css`, `admin.js` (add them to `on-load` as `%over` and to `+serve-file`). `/admin` needs the cookie: without it answer a 302 to `/~/login?redirect=/apps/register/admin` (eyre's login page) rather than 403, so an organizer who opens the link gets the login form.
- `GET /api/admin/regs` answers `{"regs": [rows], "counts": {...}, "caps": {...}, "now": ...}` with `+en-row` rows and positions from `+position-of`. Filtering, sorting and search happen in the browser.
- `GET /api/admin/reg/<rid>` as today plus `exempt`, `prior`, `position`.
- `POST /api/admin/reg/<rid>` ops, each a writer op with `by` from `admin-by`:
  - `edit {input}` and `cancel {note}` as today; `promote`; `assist {approve}`.
  - `pay {method, amount, gift, ref, note}`: an organizer records a check, cash or other payment. Allowed at `%payment` and `%assistance`; advances to `%complete` with the payment fields. Method one of `check cash other`.
  - `waiver-paper`: records `waiver [%paper '' %completed now]` and, at `%waiver`, advances by `after-waiver`. At other statuses only the waiver record changes (a `set-waiver` writer op).
  - `refund {note}`: sets `refunded=&` and appends the note to `payment.note`; history line `refunded`. Only when `payment.method` is not `%none`.
  - `exempt {on}`: sets the flag; history `marked exempt` or `exempt cleared`.
  - `reinstate`: `+reinstate`; 409 when `~`.
  - `note {notes}`: replaces `notes` (cap `max-notes`); history `notes changed`.
  - `resend {template}`: template one of `confirmation manage promoted assistance_approved assistance_declined reminder cancelled`. Phase 2 sends; here it pokes a `note` op with `what` `'email.<template>'`, `why` the filled subject, `by` `'stub'`. Never a body.
  - `recheck-waiver`: 501 `'waiver recheck is phase 2'`.
- `POST /api/admin/add {input, exempt?, paid?: {method, amount, ref, note}, waiver_paper?: bool}`: a manual registration, source `%admin`, no window or cap check, strict input. It lands at `%waiver`, then, when `waiver_paper`, at `after-waiver`, then, when `paid`, at `%complete`, all in one writer op `add` that builds the registration and its history lines (`added by organizer`, and the steps). Answers `{rid, token, status}`. Late-add accounting: `+tally` already counts admin walkers under `late`.
- `GET /api/admin/export/people.csv` and `regs.csv`: `text/csv; charset=utf-8`, `content-disposition: attachment; filename="register-people-<date>.csv"`. `GET /api/admin/export/bundle.json`: the bundle as JSON, attachment. `GET /api/admin/export/bundle.jam`: `application/octet-stream`, attachment `register-<date>.jam`, body the jammed atom as bytes (`(as-octs:mimes:html jam)` is wrong for an atom of bytes; use `[(met 3 a) a]` as the octs).
- `POST /api/admin/import` with JSON `{"jam": "<base64>"}` or `{"bundle": {...}}`, plus `?dry=1` for inspection. Decode (`de:base64:mimes:html` for jam, `+de-bundle-why` for JSON); a failure answers 400 naming why. The dry run answers `{regs, complete, waitlist, cancelled, earliest, latest, event_days, overwrite}` where `overwrite` is the count of ids already on the tree. The apply, `?wipe=0|1`, pokes the writer with a `[/register %bundle]` blot (a new noun-passthrough marc `code/mar/register/bundle.hoon`) carrying `[wipe=? by=@t =bundle]`; `+apply` accepts that blot beside `[/ %json]` and `+do-restore` writes every registration (fresh with `make-gained-soft`, existing with `over`), adds a history line `restored from backup` to each, replaces `settings.json`, `copy.json` and `counts.json`, culls every registration not in the bundle when `wipe` (`cull:io` on its road), notes one ring entry `restore` with the counts in `why`. Answers `{"ok": true, "applied": n}`.
- `GET` and `PUT /api/admin/counts`: `counts.json`, the actual counts per day per activity: `{"fri": {"mass": {"count": 0, "time": "", "note": ""}, "walk": ..., "holy_hour": ..., "social": ..., "bus": ...}, "sat": {...}, "sun": {...}}`. PUT replaces the document (`set-counts`).
- The beacon: nothing new; the writer bumps it. The backoffice reads `/grubbery/api/keep/<instance>/beacon/rev` the way orrery's page does (copy the `stream()` function).

## 3. The backoffice page (`code/nex/register/admin.html`, `admin.css`, `admin.js`)

Plain, dense, no build. Same fonts and palette tokens as the public page, a narrower type scale, tables. Header: "Register" brand, nav (Roster, Reports, Counts, Copy, Settings, Backup), the live status dot, and **"acting as Susan"** on the right: read from `localStorage['register.actor']`; when empty, the first mutating click opens a small inline prompt (a name field and a Save button, not `window.prompt`) and the action proceeds after save; a click on the name lets them change it. Every mutating fetch sends `x-actor`. A 400 `actor: required` from the ship reopens the prompt.

Views, by hash:

- `#roster` (default): a filter bar (segment select: all, complete, pending (waiver+payment+assistance), wait list, financial assistance, unpaid, unsigned, drafts, cancelled, Bambino only, non-walkers, NE Florida Order of Malta and volunteers (knight_dame or volunteer, state FL), exempt, admin adds; a search box matching name, email, phone, org; a track select), a count line ("212 of 325 pilgrims, 3 on the wait list, 5 drafts"), and the table: sortable columns (click a header: last name of the first person, state, status, track, created, fees) with an arrow, rows link to `#reg/<rid>`. All filtering client-side over `/api/admin/regs`.
- `#reg/<rid>`: two columns at desktop, one at phone. Left: the party as an editable form (the same person cards as the public form, reuse the same field list, plus contact, org, why, assistance, together, notes textarea, exempt checkbox). Save posts `edit` (and `exempt`, `note` when changed). Right: status badge, fees, payment card (method, amount, gift, at, ref, refunded, note) with "Record payment" (method select, amount prefilled with fees, ref, note) and "Mark refunded"; waiver card with "Signed on paper" and "Re-read envelope"; actions row by status: Promote (waitlist), Approve and Decline assistance (assistance), Cancel with a note (any active), Reinstate (cancelled); emails: a select of the seven templates and a Send button; the history list newest first with at, by, what. A confirm step for cancel, refund, reinstate.
- `#add`: the same party form empty, plus "exempt from caps", "paid by" (none, check, cash, other with amount and ref) and "waiver signed on paper"; Submit posts `/api/admin/add` and goes to the new registration.
- `#reports`: computed in the browser from the roster rows plus `/api/admin/counts` and `/api/status` (caps, fees): totals by track and status; counted vs cap for full and Bambino, socials vs caps, late adds vs pool; planned per day per activity from the people (walk, Mass Friday, Holy Hour, socials, bus; Sunday total across both tracks vs 350, the Shrine capacity, shown as "Sunday at the Shrine: 214 of 350"); by state (top 10, count and share); by organization; registrations per day since the first (a simple bar list, no chart library); wait list length; payment totals (sum of amount and gift by method); planned vs actual per day per activity when counts exist. Export buttons: people.csv, regs.csv.
- `#counts`: a grid of day × activity with count, time and note inputs; Save PUTs the document.
- `#copy`: every key of copy.json as a labelled textarea (grouped by prefix: landing, form, next, manage, email, other); Save PUTs the whole document; a "reset to defaults" is NOT offered (the ship has no route for it).
- `#settings`: a form over settings.json: event name and days, fees (dollars, converted to cents), caps, late adds, hold hours, window (three datetime-local inputs, stored as ISO Z), public URL, orgs (one per line), mail from and key, Stripe key, DocuSign fields, provider mode select. Masked secrets show as password fields with `****`; leaving them alone keeps the stored value (the ship's unmask). Save PUTs.
- `#backup`: Download buttons (JSON bundle, jam); an Import box: a file input (accepts .json and .jam; a .jam is read as an ArrayBuffer and base64'd), an Inspect button that POSTs `?dry=1` and shows the summary (registrations, complete, wait list, cancelled, date range, event days, how many existing would be overwritten), a "wipe registrations not in the file" checkbox, and an Apply button enabled only after an inspect, with a confirm.

Every cell is `esc()`ed. The page refreshes the roster on the beacon and every 60 seconds, and never while a form field has focus (orrery's `editing()` guard).

## 4. Gate additions (`scripts/api-matrix.py`) and a page smoke line

- `GET /admin` without the cookie is a 302 to the login page; with it 200 html. `admin.js` and `admin.css` served with the right types.
- A manual add with paid check and paper waiver lands `complete`, source `admin`, and `counts.late` rises by its walkers while `counts.full` does not.
- Exempt: mark a complete full-track party exempt; `counts.full` drops by its walkers, `social_sat` does not change.
- Reinstate: cancel a complete party (counts drop), reinstate, status `complete` again, counts back.
- Record payment on an assistance party: status `complete`, method `check`.
- Refund: `refunded` true, history has `refunded`.
- Notes and exempt through `edit`-adjacent ops; `resend` pokes a ring entry `email.reminder`.
- Exports: `people.csv` parses with Python's csv module, header count equals every row, one row per person; `regs.csv` one row per registration; `bundle.json` decodes and has the same number of regs as the roster; `bundle.jam` is bytes, non-empty.
- Import dry run of the downloaded jam answers `overwrite` equal to the roster count; apply with `wipe=0` answers `applied` and the roster is unchanged; then: cancel one registration, import the same jam again with `wipe=0`, the cancelled one is back to its bundle status (a restore overwrites by id). Never run `wipe=1` in the gate against wex's tree unless the bundle was taken in the same run and the tree has only matrix rows; skip wipe.
- Counts: PUT a document with `fri.mass.count` 120, GET it back.
- The unit tests: expect 26 or more arms.
